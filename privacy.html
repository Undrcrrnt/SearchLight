SearchLight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

SearchLight is a personal before-and-after photo comparison app for rooms and belongings. It runs on your device. It does not create an account and it does not upload your photos.

Data we collect

SearchLight does not collect personal data on our servers. We do not operate analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or cloud backup.

Photos and camera

If you grant camera access, SearchLight uses it only to take baseline and check photos that you start. If you pick a photo from your library, the system photo picker lets you choose one image. SearchLight does not request access to your entire photo library.

Photos, session names, masks, and comparison logs stay in SearchLight’s private app storage on your device until you delete that session or uninstall the app.

Sharing

If you use Export Log, you choose where that text file goes through the Android share sheet. SearchLight does not send it anywhere on its own.

Permissions

  • Camera — take photos you request

Children

SearchLight is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Use the developer contact email listed on the Google Play store listing for this app.
